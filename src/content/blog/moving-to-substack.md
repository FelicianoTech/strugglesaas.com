After 7 months, Struggle SaaS is returning with a new project and a new home.
I'll keep this website (`struggle.feliciano.tech`) up just in case but new chapters of the Struggle SaaS story will now be published over on [Substack](https://substack.com).

<!--more-->

## Struggle SaaS on Substack

If you want to follow my journey building Cinnamon Pet and the struggle leading up to it, here's what to do.
Visit [struggle.substack.com](https://struggle.substack.com).
From there you can subscribe to the blog and get posts (about every 3-5 weeks) in your inbox, or you can just view them on the website.
If you are already subscribed to the Struggle SaaS newsletter, you'll get an email confirming your invite to Substack.


## Cinnamon Pet Diary

Subscribing to Struggle SaaS on Substack will get you the same posts you have been getting here.
Thoughts, reflections, plans, etc during my journey building a SaaS.
On Substack I've also started something called Cinnamon Pet Diary.
This will be diary-like entries, almost everyday, covering my tasks and quick thoughts for that day.
Much more frequent but less text to read.
It's not for everyone but it's available as a separate newsletter if you're interested.
Just check out [Cinnamon Pet Diary](https://strugglesaas.substack.com/s/cinnamon-pet-diary) and you can subscribe to that separately.


## LinkedIn

If you're on LinkedIn and rather that instead of Substack, cool.
Stick with the link above and you're good to go.
